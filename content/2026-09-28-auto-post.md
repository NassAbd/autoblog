## 2. The Intersection of AI and Politics  

### A Growing Policy Battlefield  

AI has moved from a niche research domain to a national security priority. Governments worldwide are drafting legislation on data privacy, algorithmic transparency, and export controls. In the United States, the conversation has accelerated after high‑profile incidents involving disinformation, deepfakes, and autonomous weapons.

### Why a Former President?  

1. **Legitimacy for Policy Proposals**  
   - A former president can rally bipartisan support for legislation that might otherwise be mired in partisan gridlock.  
2. **Signal to the Industry**  
   - A meeting with a high‑profile political figure signals that the company is willing to engage in the public policy arena, potentially influencing upcoming regulations.  
3. **Strategic Positioning**  
   - By aligning with a political heavyweight, Anthropic can secure a seat at the table when federal AI funding and procurement decisions are made.

### Historical Precedents  

- **Apple & Steve Jobs (2007)** – Jobs’ meeting with President Bush helped shape early discussions on mobile security.  
- **Google & Sundar Pichai (2018)** – Pichai’s testimony before Congress set a tone for the “AI accountability” narrative.  

Anthropic’s dinner follows a pattern: tech leaders seeking political allies to navigate an increasingly regulated environment.
